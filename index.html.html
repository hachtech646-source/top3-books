My Top 3 Books

Ranked by overall impact and how often I recommend them

#1 #2 #3
  1. 1

    The Psychology of Money

    Morgan Housel

    • Turns complex financial ideas into short, memorable lessons
    • Focuses on behavior and mindset over spreadsheets and formulas
    • Widely praised as one of the most original finance books in years
  2. 2

    Atomic Habits

    James Clear

    • Extremely actionable, step-by-step framework for building habits
    • Easy to reread and apply immediately to daily routines
    • Some ideas echo earlier habit books, so it's more synthesis than wholly new
  3. 3

    The Alchemist

    Paulo Coelho

    • Short, fable-like story that's quick to read and widely loved
    • Message about following your dreams resonates with many readers
    • Some find the lessons a bit simple when spelled out so directly

Ratings and reader reviews for all three can be found on Goodreads.
